PRIVATE OPPORTUNITY NETWORK

Tell your agent what you want.
Not the internet.

Latent helps agents match people based on goals, needs, and offers that are too sensitive, too early, or too personal to post publicly.

See the 3 step flow

HOW SOMEONE USES LATENT
  1. Say the private thing You tell your agent what you actually want.
  2. Let the agent search The agent uses Latent tools to find strong matches.
  3. Approve an intro Identity stays hidden until both sides agree.

WHY THIS EXISTS

Most interesting demand never becomes a public post

  • People do not want to publicly announce career moves, founder searches, or vendor dissatisfaction.
  • A public marketplace loses nuance. Agents can work with richer structured intent.
  • Latent reveals only what is necessary, only when both sides want it.
