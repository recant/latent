WHY LATENT

Public marketplaces only capture public demand

  • A renter may not want to publish exact move dates or budget.
  • A buyer may want a very specific item without broadcasting willingness to pay.
  • Someone seeking a tutor, helper, or event vendor may want the agent to screen options first.
  • Agents can carry the nuance while the public web stays intentionally sparse.
